Dual Maps - Compare Map Views Side by Side

Dual Maps lets you compare, overlay and blend different map layers side by side. Synchronize satellite views, 3D buildings, railroads, Bing maps, topography, LIDAR and air traffic maps in real time.

Available Map Layers

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM)
  • OpenStreetMap DE
  • Humanitarian Map
  • Topography Map
  • Cycling Map
  • Satellite View
  • Railway Map
  • Lidar Map

Available Comparison Views

  • Google Maps
  • Google Street View
  • 3D Buildings (OSM Buildings)
  • Bing Maps
  • Airplanes Radar (ADS-B)

Dual Maps – Compare Map Views Side by Side

Dual Maps lets you compare different map types side by side. Synchronize satellite views, 3D buildings, railroads, Bing maps, topography, and air traffic, Lidar maps in real time.

Available Map Layers

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM)
  • OpenStreetMap DE
  • Humanitarian Map
  • Topography Map
  • Cycling Map
  • Satellite View
  • Railway Map
  • Lidar Map

Available Comparison Views

  • Google Maps
  • Google Street View
  • 3D Buildings (OSM Buildings)
  • Bing Maps
  • Waze
  • Airplanes Radar (ADS-B)
  • UMP
